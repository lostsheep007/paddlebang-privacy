Paddlebang privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Paddlebang is a free game for Android, published by BoomTime4 Pty Ltd ("we").

The short version

Paddlebang does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. Everything the game does happens on your phone.

Permissions

Paddlebang requests no device permissions.

What we don't do

  • The game has no internet access, and sends nothing from your phone to us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No third-party services are built into the game.

Settings

The game remembers a few choices on your phone, such as music and sound volume and whether you have seen the tutorial. They never leave your phone, and uninstalling the game removes them.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
